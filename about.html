About Us

The Physical Education Unit of the University of Ruhuna is a dynamic and progressive center dedicated to promoting health, performance, and holistic student development through structured physical education and competitive sports.

In today’s rapidly evolving academic environment, physical well-being and mental resilience are essential components of student success. The Unit plays a vital role in cultivating physically active, emotionally balanced, and socially responsible graduates who demonstrate leadership, teamwork, discipline, and respect for institutional and national values.

We provide a structured platform for both recreational participation and high-performance sports training. Through scientifically guided coaching, organized competitions, and inclusive fitness programs, students are empowered to develop their athletic potential while maintaining academic excellence.

The University is equipped with a modern Gymnasium recognized among the leading university indoor sports complexes in Sri Lanka with a seating capacity of 1,200 spectators. The facility supports multiple indoor sports disciplines and serves as a venue for inter-faculty and inter-university competitions.

The University Playground, commissioned in 1998, continues to function as a central hub for outdoor sports training and major sporting events. Additionally, faculties located outside the main campus are facilitated with accessible indoor and outdoor sports facilities to ensure inclusive participation across the University community.

The Physical Education Unit remains committed to advancing university sports through innovation, professionalism, and a student-centered approach shaping well-rounded graduates prepared to lead both on and off the field.

Our Vision

To become a leading university in sports excellence and encourage students to achieve their goals.

To develop students' sports skills, teamwork, leadership and physical fitness through sports activities.

Our Objectives

To promote sports participation, organize competitions and provide opportunities for students to develop their talents.

Message from the Chairman, Sports Advisory Board





Sports play a vital role in our life, significantly contributing to both physical and mental well-being. Regular participation in sports enhances physical fitness, strengthens personality, and builds self-confidence among undergraduates. Moreover, engaging in sports provides a great advantage in reducing stress, improving concentration, and improving academic performance. Beyond health benefits, sports cultivate essential life skills such as teamwork, leadership, discipline, resilience, and time management. These attributes are invaluable for students’ personal development and future professional success. Participation in sports also fosters a strong sense of community, encouraging friendship, unity, and university spirit while promoting a balanced and dynamic lifestyle.

Accordingly, sports are not merely extracurricular activities; they are a powerful force that shapes character, strengthens resilience, and inspires excellence. At university, where young minds are nurtured for future leadership, engagement in sports plays a transformative role in building both physical vitality and mental strength.

A vibrant sports culture is the heartbeat of a dynamic university. Fostering a vibrant sports culture at the university is essential as it unites students beyond classrooms, fosters friendships, strengthens university spirit, and creates a balanced and energetic academic environment. When students step onto the field, they are not only competing but they are growing, discovering their potential, and preparing themselves for the greater challenges of life.

As Chairman of the Sports Advisory Board, I wholeheartedly encourage every student to take an active role in our sporting activities. Join hands with the Physical Education Unit of the University of Ruhuna and embrace the opportunity to lead a healthy, disciplined, and fulfilling university life. Let us build together a strong sporting tradition that enriches our university and contributes to the prosperity of the nation.

Message from the Director of Physical Education





It is a distinct honour to serve as the Director of the Physical Education Unit of the University of Ruhuna. The Unit carries a significant responsibility in fostering the physical, mental, and social well-being of our students, thereby contributing to the broader mission of the University in developing competent, disciplined, and responsible graduates.

Physical education and sport play an essential role in higher education. Beyond enhancing physical fitness, structured sporting engagement cultivates leadership, discipline, teamwork, perseverance, and respect for diversity. These attributes are fundamental in shaping individuals who are capable of contributing meaningfully to society and upholding national values with integrity and commitment.

The Physical Education Unit is committed to providing well-organized training programmes, competitive sporting opportunities, and inclusive recreational activities for all students. We maintain high standards of professionalism in coaching, event management, and sports administration, while continuously seeking opportunities for development and innovation.

Our objective is to create a dynamic and inclusive sporting environment that encourages excellence, fair play, and personal growth. I warmly invite all students to actively engage with the programmes offered by the Unit and to embrace sport as an integral component of university life.

Together, let us continue to strengthen the sporting culture of the University of Ruhuna and promote holistic student development.
